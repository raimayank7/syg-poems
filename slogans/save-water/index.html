Save water slogans

A water slogan should be short enough to shout at a rally and clear enough for a child to explain. Most are our own lines, written for posters, banners and morning assembly. The well-known ones are grouped separately at the end.

World Water Day is observed on 22 March every year.

English

  • Close the tap. Open the future.

  • Save water today, or search for it tomorrow.

  • A leaking tap wastes more than water.

  • Use a bucket, not a hose.

  • Water is life. Do not pour life down the drain.

  • Catch the rain where it falls.

  • Think before you turn the tap.

  • No water, no us.

  • Little drops make full tanks.

  • Rivers do not come with refills.

  • Be water wise, not water sorry.

  • The cheapest water is the water you do not waste.

  • A full bucket today, a full well tomorrow.

  • Turn it off while you brush. That is all we ask.

  • Rain is free. Catch it.

  • The tap listens to your hand. Be kind.

  • Every drop you save is still here next summer.

  • Fix the drip before it becomes a flood of regret.

Where a water slogan works best

A water slogan does the most good at the point where water is used: above the school taps, beside the water cooler, on the society notice board near the pump timings, on the tanker, at the gate of a car wash. For rallies and prabhat pheris choose the rhyming Hindi or Punjabi lines, because a crowd can keep time with them. For a poster competition choose a line with a picture already inside it, such as a tap, a drop or an empty pot.

Write your own water slogan

Start from one action, not from "save water" in general. Close the tap while brushing. Fix the leak. Use a bucket in place of a hose. Water plants in the evening. Catch the rain. Then pair the action with what it protects: tomorrow, the well, the fields, your children.

  • Useful English pairs: drop and stop, drain and rain, waste and haste, save and behave, pour and more.
  • Useful Hindi pairs: पानी and ज़िंदगानी, जल and कल, बूँद and मूँद, बचाओ and अपनाओ, नल and हल.
  • Useful Punjabi pairs: ਪਾਣੀ and ਕਹਾਣੀ, ਜਲ and ਕੱਲ੍ਹ, ਬੂੰਦ and ਮੂੰਦ.

Poster idea

Draw one large drop in the centre and put a small scene inside it: a child drinking, a green field or a bird at a bowl. Write the slogan above in thick blue letters and one action below in smaller letters. Leave white space around the drop. A crowded chart is hard to read from the judges' table.

What to avoid

  • Numbers and statistics you have not checked. A wrong figure on a chart loses marks.
  • Blaming one group, such as farmers, factories or cities. Water is saved by everyone or by no one.

Using these slogans

  • On a poster, put the slogan in the largest letters and one simple drawing below it: a tap, a drop, a bucket.
  • Add one action people can take: "Fix the leaking tap today."
