Where these slogans are used

Enrolment rallies at the start of the session, literacy day programmes, library week, adult literacy classes, and the wall beside the school gate that every parent passes twice a day. Many are also used as headings for essays and speeches.

Write your own

Education slogans are addressed to two different people. To a child, speak of what learning opens: books, questions, wings, a job, a wider world. To a parent, speak of what schooling protects: a daughter's future, a son's choices, the family's name. Decide who will read your line before you write it. For an enrolment rally in a village, write in the language spoken at home there, and say the school's name.

  • English pairs: read and lead, learn and earn, school and tool, book and look, write and bright.
  • Hindi pairs: पढ़ो and बढ़ो, शिक्षा and दीक्षा, ज्ञान and सम्मान, किताब and जवाब, पाठशाला and उजाला.
  • Punjabi pairs: ਪੜ੍ਹੋ and ਵਧੋ, ਗਿਆਨ and ਮਾਣ, ਕਿਤਾਬ and ਜਵਾਬ, ਸਕੂਲ and ਅਸੂਲ.

Ideas that go with the slogan

  • A "each one, teach one" pledge board where students sign up to help one younger child read.
  • A rally stop outside homes of children who are not in school, with a welcome, not a scolding.
  • A book donation box under the slogan.

What to avoid

  • Mocking people who cannot read. Many never had the chance.
  • Slogans that value only marks and ranks. Learning is wider than a report card.

Using these slogans

  • For an enrolment rally, add the school's name and admission dates under the slogan.
